\# External Readability Policy (Kernel Next)



This document defines how this repository must remain readable to

non-technical, external audiences.



Readability is a first-class requirement.



---



\## Core Principle



If an external reviewer cannot understand intent without explanation,

the repository has failed.



---



\## Language Rules



\- Plain English only

\- No internal jargon without definition

\- No assumed technical knowledge

\- No acronyms without expansion



---



\## Structural Rules



\- Documents must be short and scoped

\- Each document must have a single purpose

\- Entry points must be obvious



---



\## What Is Not Allowed



\- Implicit knowledge

\- Cross-repo assumptions

\- Hidden dependencies

\- “Read this later” placeholders



---



\## Responsibility



Maintaining readability is mandatory.

Complexity must be justified or removed.



---



End of document.
